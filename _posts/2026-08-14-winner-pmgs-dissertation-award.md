We are delighted to announce **Charlotte Rulkens** as the winner of the **2026 Paul Meehl Graduate School Dissertation Award!**

Her dissertation, **“Rembrandt and Rubens Revisited: Towards more Transparent and Replicable Attributions,”** has been selected as the winning dissertation for this edition of the award. We warmly congratulate Charlotte on this achievement and are very pleased to recognize her work with this award.

As this year’s award winner, Charlotte will join us as one of the keynote speakers at the Paul Meehl Graduate School Meta-Research Symposium in October, where she will present her dissertation and share her work with our community.

We were delighted to receive so many high-quality applications for this edition of the award. We would like to sincerely thank all applicants for submitting their work and for their interest in the Paul Meehl Graduate School Dissertation Award.

**Join us at the Meta-Research Symposium in October**

The Paul Meehl Graduate School Meta-Research Symposium will take place on **October 8–9, 2026**, at Eindhoven University of Technology. We look forward to bringing together researchers from across the meta-research community for two days of presentations, discussions, and exchange.

The **final program** is now available here: [Program](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kvmTqjEpbBOmCRjl5WF82sTFGk4cO25r3CXSq5yu1OY/edit?usp=sharing).

Registration is open until **September 30, 2026**. You can register for the symposium by completing the [Registration Form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=R_J9zM5gD0qddXBM9g78ZP_Kihp-VglPgWom9gajHXdUQUFPOFVZQUUwN1lNWlRWRlVRNklIQVBKRi4u).

We look forward to welcoming you to Eindhoven in October and to celebrating this year’s award winner together.
