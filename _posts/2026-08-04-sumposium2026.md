We are delighted to announce that registration is now open for the **third Paul Meehl Graduate School Meta-Research Symposium**!

In collaboration with the **Platform for Young Meta-Scientists (PYMS)**, we are organizing a **two-day event** on **October 8–9, 2026**, at **Eindhoven University of Technology**. The program includes a half-day workshop, followed by hackathon sessions and a social activity organized by PYMS on **October 8**, and a full day of presentations and keynote talks on **October 9**.

A preliminary version of the program is available here: [Preliminary Program](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1kvmTqjEpbBOmCRjl5WF82sTFGk4cO25r3CXSq5yu1OY/edit?usp=sharing).

You can register for the symposium by **September 30, 2026**, by completing the following registration form: [Registration Form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=R_J9zM5gD0qddXBM9g78ZP_Kihp-VglPgWom9gajHXdUQUFPOFVZQUUwN1lNWlRWRlVRNklIQVBKRi4u)
 
The symposium brings together early-career researchers and established experts to exchange ideas, build community, and strengthen networks in the field of meta-research.

We are honored to welcome **Dr. Joeri Tijdink** (Amsterdam UMC) as our keynote speaker. Our second keynote speaker will be the recipient of the **Paul Meehl Graduate School Dissertation Award**, who will be announced by mid-August.

Find out more by visiting the webpage for the event here: [Paul Meehl Graduate School Meta Research Symposium 2026](https://paulmeehlschool.github.io/workshops/symposium2026/)

We look forward to welcoming you to Eindhoven this October!
