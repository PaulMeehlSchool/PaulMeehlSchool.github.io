We are excited to announce that registration is now open for the first workshop
of the new academic year, “Evaluating Predictive Accuracy”. In this session, Benny Markovitch will focus on 
the principles of machine learning models. He will focus on the understanding and addressing the fundamental pitfalls of overfitting, data leakage, and distribution shifts and other challenges for evaluating the predictive accuracy of the models.

For further details about this workshop, click [here](https://paulmeehlschool.github.io/workshops/predicting-accuracy/). Please note that the workshop will be **exclusively in-person** on **November 27, 2026**. We look forward to seeing you in Eindhoven!

You can register for this workshop by completing the [Registration Form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=R_J9zM5gD0qddXBM9g78ZP_Kihp-VglPgWom9gajHXdUNVpCRUxTS0w3TUlKM0hZVUxRWVRJMkRLUS4u). 
Please make sure to register before <ins>**November 10**</ins>.  The registration link will remain open after this date if spots are still available.
