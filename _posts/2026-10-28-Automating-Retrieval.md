We are excited to announce that registration is now open for the workshop, "Automating Retrieval and Textual Analysis of Scientific Papers". 
In this session, four speakers will discuss how extracting structured knowledge and systematic reviews can be automatized and what to consider in this way.
The morning session will be led by Rasoul Norouzi and Erik Tjong Kim Sang where they discuss about retrieving papers and making transparent pipelines for extracting knowledge,
and the afternoon session will be led by Dirk Wulff and Daniel Lakens, discussing conceptual clarity and harmonization and using MetaCheck for systematic reviews.

For further details about this workshop, click [here](https://paulmeehlschool.github.io/workshops/text-mining/). Please note that the workshop will be **exclusively in-person** on **December 16, 2026**. 
We look forward to seeing you in Eindhoven!
